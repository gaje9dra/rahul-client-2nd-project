  • Browse individual interests before reaching out.

Need to know

Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers before you reach out.

How do the contact buttons work?

WhatsApp opens a new chat with a pre-filled greeting, while Call opens the phone dialler on compatible devices. The demo numbers must be replaced before live use.

Are the profiles and contact details real?

No. This static sample uses placeholder profile copy, stock portrait photos, and dummy contact numbers for design demonstration. It does not verify identity or availability.

Can I find profiles by interest?

Yes. Use the filter chips to narrow the cards by interest, or type a name or interest into the search field.

Is this website taking bookings or payments?

No. This is a front-end-only static page. It has no account system, payment flow, booking engine, or backend to store messages.
